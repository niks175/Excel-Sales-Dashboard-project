# Excel-Sales-Dashboard-Project

I built this dashboard in Excel to get a quick, one-page view of how a sales business is performing across Europe. It shows revenue, profit, orders and more, and you can filter everything with slicers.

![Dashboard preview](executive%20sales%20dashboard.png)

## What the dashboard shows

At the top, six cards give the headline numbers:

- Total revenue: $645,223.18
- Total gross profit: $296,384.25
- Gross profit margin: 46%
- Total units sold: 6,698
- Total orders: 2,707
- COGS: $348,838.93

Below the cards are the charts:

- **Revenue and profit by month:** a line chart that shows how sales and gross profit move through the year
- **Top 5 countries by revenue:** a bar chart of the best-performing countries
- **Region-wise comparison:** a donut chart of revenue by region
- **Country-wise map:** a map of Europe shaded by revenue
- **Revenue share by channel:** a pie chart of Corporate, Marketplace, Online Store and Retail Partners

On the right, slicers let you filter by product category, country and quarter, and every chart updates when you do.

## What I found

- Revenue is highest in June (about $70K) and lowest in February (about $36K).
- The United Kingdom ($74K) and Germany ($70K) bring in the most revenue, followed by France, Italy and Spain.
- The Online Store is the biggest sales channel at 40% of revenue, ahead of Corporate at 28%.
- Western Europe (31%) and Central Europe (30%) make up the largest share of revenue by region.
- Gross profit margin is 46%, which means almost half of every sale is left after the cost of goods.

## How I built it

I cleaned the sales data, built PivotTables for each view, and turned them into charts. Then I added slicers and arranged everything on a single dashboard sheet so it is easy to read at a glance. I used Excel's PivotTables, PivotCharts, slicers and map chart.

## Files

- `Excel Sales Dashboard.xlsx`: the full workbook with the data, pivot tables and dashboard
- `executive sales dashboard.png`: a screenshot of the finished dashboard

## How to use it

1. Download the Excel file.
2. Open it and go to the dashboard sheet.
3. Click the slicers on the right to filter by product category, country or quarter.

## What I learned

This project helped me practise building PivotTables, choosing the right chart for each question, and laying out a dashboard so a manager can understand it in a few seconds.
